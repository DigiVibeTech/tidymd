## Description
TidyMD turns the page you're reading into tidy Markdown in one click. Paste it into Obsidian, Notion, GitHub or your favourite AI chat.

What's free:
- Copy article as Markdown. Menus, footers and ads are removed automatically.
- Copy full page, or download as a .md file
- Right-click: Copy selection as Markdown
- Tables come out as real GFM tables, even tables without a header row and cells with several paragraphs
- Code blocks keep their line breaks and language (```js, ```py ...)
- Links and images are made absolute, so they still work after you paste

TidyMD Pro ($2.99/month or $19/year):
- Your own templates, plus a YAML front matter preset (title, source, date)
- All open tabs merged into one Markdown file, which is great for research and prompts
- Send to Obsidian (new note in your vault)

Privacy: everything is converted on your device. TidyMD has no account, no analytics, and never sends page content anywhere. Payments for Pro are processed by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

## Privacy practices (Chrome dashboard answers)
- **Single purpose:** convert the current web page or selection to Markdown.
- **Permission justifications:**
  - activeTab and scripting: read the page you clicked on to convert it
  - clipboardWrite: copy the result
  - downloads: save .md files
  - storage: your template settings
  - contextMenus: the right-click action
  - extensionpay.com host: the Pro payment status
- **Data collected:** none. (Pro buyers give their email and payment details to ExtensionPay/Stripe, not to us.)
- **Not sold, not used for unrelated purposes, not used for creditworthiness.** Certify all three.
- **Firefox:** `data_collection_permissions: required ["none"]` is set in the manifest.
